# RAE — Broker Reconciliation SOP

Purpose: connect RAE acquisition data to outcomes that are actually verified in partner back offices.

## Daily reconciliation
1. Open Deriv partner reporting and HFM Partner Area.
2. Record only newly verified outcomes: broker signup, first deposit, first trade, active trader, commission.
3. Enter them in RAE #admin under the correct broker.
4. Add a short non-personal note when useful (date/report period). Do not enter client names, phone numbers, emails, account IDs or other PII.
5. Refresh the dashboard and compare traffic/click movement with verified outcomes.

## Rules
- Never infer a signup/deposit/trade from a click.
- Never fabricate missing broker data.
- Do not duplicate the same reporting-period outcome.
- Commission amount must come from the broker report.
- Funnel attribution is directional until the broker provides a reliable referral-level mapping.

## Daily decision
Use RAE traffic metrics to optimize acquisition experience. Use broker back-office data to judge actual business outcomes. Keep the two layers explicitly separate.
